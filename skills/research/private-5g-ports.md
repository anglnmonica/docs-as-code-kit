# Research library: private 5G in ports and container terminals

Verified public findings, for the research-doc skill to reuse before it
searches again. Every finding links to the page that was read. Check that
a source is still current before you use it, and add new findings at the
end with the next number.

Last updated: 2026-11-17.

| 9 | At a large Antwerp terminal, reflections off metal containers, interference from reefer zones and docked ships, and moving vehicles made dropouts hard to avoid. | 12 | At Singapore's Pasir Panjang Terminal, automated yard cranes needed at least 105 Mbps uplink and 50 ms latency. The trial reached 140 Mbps uplink and beat the latency target. | 13 | In the same trial, automated guided vehicles reached a minimum latency of 10 ms on 3.5 GHz 5G, half of 4G. Container stacking and antenna placement strongly affected mmWave coverage. | Same | Government | 2022 | Trial. |
| 14 | A UK government-funded 5G trial at Felixstowe met remote-crane latency and stability targets in early tests but couldn't meet them consistently, so the crane wasn't operated over 5G. | 15 | In the same trial, crane sensors produced about 4 GB per crane per session for predictive maintenance. | 16 | The Felixstowe report says radio planning was needed to work around the problems containers cause, that public 5G focuses on urban areas while ports are often outside them, and that device availability, spectrum licensing, and skills are barriers. | Same, pp. 6–7 | Government-funded report | 2023 | UK context. |
| 17 | In a 2025 live pilot at a European terminal, private 5G standalone carried remote RTG and RMG crane operation with about 24 ms average end-to-end latency against a 20 ms target, and operators reported stable control. ## Claims not to use

- 30–120 Mbps uplink per crane, about 5 Mbps per HD camera, and up to 60
  gantry cranes per square kilometer: stated in an analyst paper with no
  source.
- One port replacing 50 Wi-Fi access points with two cellular radios:
  vendor-sourced, with no independent confirmation.
- No independent source has published measured return on investment from
  a port private 5G deployment, as of the last update.
