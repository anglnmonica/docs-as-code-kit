Alerts let you know when something goes wrong on your network.

## Overview

The Relay Gateway can send alerts by email or SNMP. To set them up, click
here and follow the steps below.

## Steps

1. Go to Settings and click the blue Notifications button on the right.
2. Turn on alerts and enter your email address. It sends a test message.
3. Set the threshold. The default is 80%.
4. Click Save. This can't be changed later without resetting the gateway.

## Troubleshooting

If you don't get emails, check it again.
